Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Auto Scheduler is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read the connected channel's name. It requests no other Google data.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens, videos and video details stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to YouTube when a video is uploaded.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Auto Scheduler's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
